QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Pitch Stage FAQ

What is Pitch Stage?

A structured opportunity to present your business to investors, business leaders and decision-makers.

Who can participate?

Ambitious founders, entrepreneurs, startups and growing businesses with a clear opportunity to present.

What is the pitch format?

Five minutes for an elevator pitch, followed by three minutes of investor Q&A and subsequent evaluation.

How long is the pitch?

The stage format is eight minutes in total: five minutes of presentation and three minutes of Q&A.

What is the registration fee?

The Pitch Stage registration fee is ₹1,999. Payment integration is currently coming soon.

Is funding guaranteed?

Funding is not guaranteed. Selected businesses may explore potential investment opportunities from the overall funding pool, subject to investor evaluation, eligibility and selection.

How will investment opportunities work?

Eligible selected businesses may continue conversations with relevant investors. Any funding decision is made by investors after evaluation and selection.

What should I prepare before pitching?

Prepare a concise business story covering the problem, solution, market, progress, growth vision and investment readiness.
